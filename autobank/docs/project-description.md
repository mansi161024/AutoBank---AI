# AutoBank AI - Project Overview

AutoBank AI is an autonomous, intelligent personal finance and enterprise banking platform powered by multi-agent AI systems.

## Key Features
1. **Autonomous Personal Financial Advisor**: Real-time spending insights, AI recommendations, and portfolio optimization.
2. **Dynamic Budget Management**: Automated tracking, velocity alerts, and smart budget limits.
3. **Bill Automation & Reminders**: Recurring bill detection, payment scheduling, and auto-pay execution.
4. **Smart Loan & EMI Engine**: Amortization planning, debt snow-balling, and refinancing suggestions.
5. **Survival & Emergency Runway Engine**: Calculates runway in months based on liquid cash reserves vs burn rate.
6. **AI Document & KYC Verifier**: Automated parsing of ID cards, tax forms, and bank statements.
7. **Government Schemes Matchmaker**: Intelligent discovery of subsidies, grants, and social welfare programs.
